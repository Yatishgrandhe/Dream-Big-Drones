# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

People and organizations looking for a professional drone partner, from homeowners and event planners to businesses with a visual story to tell. They arrive on mobile or desktop wanting to quickly understand the work and request a tailored quote.

## Product Purpose

Dream Big Drones by RLM turns ambitious aerial ideas into polished photo and video experiences. The initial site builds confidence, introduces the service range, and makes booking a conversation simple while the portfolio grows.

## Positioning

Every flight should make a bigger story feel possible.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: schedule a discovery call.
- Secondary CTA: explore services and request custom pricing.
- Visitor takeaway: vivid aerial storytelling with a personal, professional partner.
- Proof on hand: supplied Dream Big Drones by RLM visual identity asset at `public/dream-big-drones-hero.png`; portfolio photography is forthcoming.

## Brand Personality

Optimistic, cinematic, and assured. The site should feel like a clear day before takeoff: imaginative without being whimsical, premium without feeling distant.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic drone-store interface, dense enterprise dashboard language, and the cold blue-neon aesthetic common to technical UAV sites.

## Design Principles

1. Let the supplied image establish an immediate sense of possibility.
2. Pair imaginative color with calm, legible information.
3. Make every step toward a custom quote feel personal and low-friction.
4. Use motion to suggest flight, not to distract from booking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use a WCAG 2.2 AA contrast target, visible keyboard focus, accessible landmarks, and a reduced-motion experience. All future portfolio media needs meaningful alt text.
